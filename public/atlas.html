01 · Structural architecture

Income security and capital are separate engines.

The indexed PSS pension funds the permanent income floor. The three-pool structure places only the transfer-balance headroom into pension phase and holds the remainder in an invested Pool C for growth, estate and planned-reserve flexibility; it is not a cash account.

06 · Tax and estate protection

Tax efficiency is a governed implementation problem—not a chart decoration.

The NCC wash remains a specialist strategy with eligibility, component and execution controls. Each source uses its own direct CSC lump components; washing is capped by the remaining original PSS lump and never assigns those components to Hostplus.

08 · Evidence and model governance

Every important figure carries a source class and a limit.

The Atlas now uses the same rail definitions and annual three-pool ledger as the Command Centre. It does not claim that the working age-60 Hostplus projection or future returns are facts.

EXACT

Supplied / documented

PSS iEstimator values, rail-specific lump sums, pension figures, TBC arithmetic and direct calculations.

ESTIMATED

Provider / market dependent

External implementation costs, property pricing and future Hostplus balance when based on current projections.

MODELLED

Scenario engine

Capital trajectories, spending trade-offs, estate outcomes, Pool C drag and gross-salary equivalents.

SPECULATIVE

Future state

Returns, sequence, longevity, future legislation, health costs, beneficiary status and actual inheritance tax outcomes.
