01·Floor & Fizz

The floor is guaranteed.
Everything above it is choice.

The net pension is bedrock — flat, permanent, real. Just above it runs the gross line; the sliver between them is the over-60 tax offset. Higher still, the account-based pension's mandatory minimum draws alone lift spendable income into the $100k–$110k band from day one — without ever spending capital on purpose. Turn the dial; the living layer breathes, the floor does not.

03·Death-benefits Tax

A liability, dismantled
one wash at a time.

Left alone, the taxable component of super would hand the estate a bill on the way out. The wash removes it in steps — each cycle saving a fixed $15,683.86 — until death-benefits tax reaches zero by the late sixties, in every return scenario. It never comes back.
