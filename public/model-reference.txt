POST-RETIREMENT OPERATIONAL LEDGER
mandatoryDraw = openingPoolA x ageBasedMinimumRate
lifestyleGap = max(0, targetTotalSpending - netPssPension)
actualDraw = min(openingPoolA, max(mandatoryDraw, lifestyleGap))
netIncomeAvailable = netPssPension + actualDraw
fundedSpend = min(targetTotalSpending, netIncomeAvailable)
shortfall = max(0, targetTotalSpending - netIncomeAvailable)
reinvestment = max(0, netIncomeAvailable - targetTotalSpending)
poolCDrag = openingPoolC x 0.0035
closingPoolA = max(0, openingPoolA x (1 + realReturn) - actualDraw)
closingPoolC = max(0, openingPoolC x (1 + realReturn) - poolCDrag + reinvestment)

This treatment matters for 100% pension. At net spending targets of $90k, $100k, $110k or $130k, voluntary portfolio draw is $0 and the PSS surplus is respectively $53,104.26, $43,104.26, $33,104.26 or $13,104.26 a year before other events. The surplus is invested in Pool C; it is not silently classified as spending.

V23 has two spending contexts:
1. A Command Centre shared target (including its visual explorer) is total net spending. V23 derives either the portfolio gap or the PSS surplus.
2. Manual V23 age bands are gap-above-PSS controls. Editing a band clears the imported total target. Each band maps to its own lifestyle phase, and the display subdivides a phase whenever its funding routine changes.

The birthday-year planning rows apply the ABP minimum rate for age at the preceding 1 July: under 65 4%; 65-74 5%; 75-79 6%; 80-84 7%; 85-89 9%; 90-94 11%; 95+ 14%. The provider calculates actual financial-year minima and first-year pro-rating.

RETURNS AND RISK
- Retirement projections use real dollars and the selected post-retirement real return.
- Pool C also applies a separate 0.35% annual modelled distribution drag.
- Pre-retirement Hostplus sensitivities are separately labelled nominal.
- Command Centre Monte Carlo uses 600 reproducible seeded paths and 12% volatility.
- V23 exposes run count, mean return, volatility, target age and guardrails.
- Percentiles and sampled success frequencies are stress tests, not forecasts.
- Build validation requires every zero-volatility stochastic path and its median to equal the deterministic annual ledger for every Rail B election, multiple spending targets and both displayed tax years.

TAX AND SALARY EQUIVALENCE

It excludes offsets, Medicare variations or surcharge, HELP, deductions and household effects. It is a comparison, not a tax-return estimate.
