## High-impact corrections

- The Command Centre election help now recognises four direct elections in both CSC bases and the later 9 September prudent 100% source.
- V23's Overview guide now distinguishes 1 and 9 September source dates. The welcome text counts all 33 pages.
- The Scenario comparison heading and guide now agree with the renderer: deltas are Alternative B minus Current A.
- The Pre-60 tracker and Annual recalibration guides now state that their displayed sensitivities do not replace the saved retirement plan. The old recalibration page note claiming downstream updates was corrected.
- Guardrail, trigger and annual-review language now distinguishes advisory prompts from automatic spending changes. The Adaptive Policy Comparator is opt-in and leaves the saved plan unchanged.
- Monte Carlo, longevity, sequence, stress, breakeven and health-event explanations now describe model samples or selected sensitivities. They no longer present a target-age floor pass as proof of safety throughout retirement.
- NCC wash guidance no longer asserts personal bring-forward ineligibility or treats a modelled cap as a verified entitlement. Estate wording distinguishes sourced PSS components from unresolved Hostplus components.
- The Pool C ETF guide now explains that its displayed CGT rate is illustrative and is not applied to the age-95 lines.
- Command Centre comparison labels now present the central example as a reference, with spending, funded outcome and later capital read together.

## Reading order for a non-specialist

1. Check the active rail, provider basis, election and source date.
2. Choose a spending target; inspect what was actually funded and any shortfall.
3. Inspect investment capital and gross estate separately. The home is not included in investment checks.
4. Use paired or sampled risk views as sensitivities, reading any spending cuts alongside higher capital.
5. Refresh actual balances and current rules before recording a real decision.
